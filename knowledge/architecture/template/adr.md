# NNNN. Title in the form of a decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names or roles
- **Related:** links to other ADRs, tickets, or docs

## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces are at play (team size, expected lifespan,
domain complexity, existing constraints)? Describe the situation neutrally, including
facts that cut against the decision.

## Decision

What we decided, stated in active voice. "We will ..." rather than "It was decided ...".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what follow-up work is required.
Include both positive and negative outcomes.

## Alternatives considered

Each option with a one-line reason it was not chosen.
